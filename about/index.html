About Beat the Pile

We're two Virginia Tech students. We built Beat the Pile because finding an internship shouldn't come down to who you know or how many job sites you can keep open at once.

Why "the pile"

Every popular posting gets buried under a pile of applications, and big job sites are full of listings that are old, reposted, or not real. The students who get interviews usually apply early, straight on the company's own site.

So that's what we do. Our robots read each company's own job board, and we only show a board after proving it belongs to that company. Every job links you straight to the employer's application.

For every student

Beat the Pile is for every major, not just engineers: internships first, plus entry-level, part-time and job shadowing. Students never pay. Pro features are free during beta, and the first 150 students keep them free for life.

What we promise

  • Every listing comes from the employer's own job board, never a copy from another job site.
  • Every number on the site is real and live, or it says where it came from.
  • We don't sell your data or show you ads.
  • Your profile is yours. You choose who sees it, and you can delete everything in one click.
