Questions

Good to know

Who can use it?

Companies hiring interns or new grads. You confirm a work email at your company's domain, and we check every company before it can contact students.

Do I need to post my internships?

No. We read your careers site directly. If your openings aren't showing up, use Feedback (bottom right) and send us your careers page link.

Which students can I see?

Only students who made their profile public and turned on "Let employers find me." You see what they chose to show: name, school, major, graduation year, skills, experience and projects. Never their email, phone or private application details.

How do I contact a student?

Once your company is verified, message them in the app or ask them to join your orbit. They can reply, accept or ignore. You can also meet them at career fairs.

Are you affiliated with a university?

No. Beat the Pile is an independent, student-built product. Career fair dates come from each school's public calendar.
