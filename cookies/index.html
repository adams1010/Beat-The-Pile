Cookie Policy

What we store in your browser, what loads from other services, and how to change your choice. Last updated November 12, 2026.

The short version. We use cookies and browser storage the site needs to work, like keeping you signed in and remembering your saved jobs. The only optional one is PostHog analytics, which counts page visits and taps without your name or email. No advertising cookies, no ad networks, and we don't sell your data.

Who sees the cookie banner

  • Visitors in Europe (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland), going by your device's time zone: analytics stays off until you tap Accept analytics.
  • Everyone else: analytics is on by default, and you can turn it off any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, analytics stays off unless you turn it on yourself.
  • Your choice is saved in this browser (btp_consent). Clearing your browser data resets it.

Outside services that load on our pages

These don't set cookies for us, but your browser contacts them, so they receive your IP address and normal browser details:

  • Google Fonts: the Geist font.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and unpkg: code libraries for reading resume files, making PDFs, and running the homepage.
  • Company logos: loaded from each company's own website or Google's public icon service.
  • MailerLite: only if you type your email into the homepage quiz and choose to save it.

We don't embed videos, maps, social media widgets, or ads from other companies.

Controlling cookies yourself

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the necessary ones, sign-in and saved jobs won't work.
