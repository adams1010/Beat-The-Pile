Refund Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

Beat the Pile doesn't charge anything right now. No payment system exists on the site, and Pro features are free during the beta. There's nothing to refund. If anyone asks you to pay to use Beat the Pile, or to pay for a job listed here, it isn't us. Please tell us with the Feedback button.

If paid plans are added later

  • We'll show the price, what you get, and how renewal works before you pay. Paying will always be your choice.
  • Subscriptions can be canceled at any time, and canceling stops the next charge.
  • The full refund terms will be posted on this page before the first payment is ever taken. They'll include any refund rights that the law where you live gives you.
  • Founding members keep every Pro feature at no charge, as described in the Terms of Use.

Employers

Employer accounts are not charged either. If that changes, prices and refund terms will be agreed in writing before any charge.

Questions: use the Feedback button at the bottom right of every page.
