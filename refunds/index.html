Refund Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Students are never charged. Pro features are free during the beta. The only thing we sell is career fair packages to verified employers (below). If anyone asks you to pay to use Beat the Pile, or to pay for a job listed here, it isn't us. Please tell us with the Feedback button.

If paid plans are added later

  • We'll show the price, what you get, and how renewal works before you pay. Paying will always be your choice.
  • Subscriptions can be canceled at any time, and canceling stops the next charge.
  • The full refund terms will be posted on this page before the first payment is ever taken. They'll include any refund rights that the law where you live gives you.
  • Founding members keep every Pro feature at no charge, as described in the Terms of Use.

Employers: career fair packages

  • Verified companies can buy a package that features them on a career fair's page in Career Fair Mode. Price and what's included are shown on the Packages page before you pay. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number.
  • Full refund if you cancel before your placement goes live. We put placements live within one business day of payment.
  • Once it's live, it's not refundable, except: if the fair is canceled, or we fail to show your placement as described, we refund in full.
  • A package is placement on Beat the Pile only. It isn't registration for the fair itself, which each school runs.
  • To cancel or ask for a refund, email support@beatthepile.com with your company name and the fair.

Questions: support@beatthepile.com, or the Feedback button at the bottom right of every page.
