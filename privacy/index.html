Privacy, in plain words

What we keep, where it lives, what goes to AI, who can see your resume, and how to delete all of it. Last updated November 2026.

Browsing needs nothing

You can browse every internship without an account or an email. We count page visits and button taps with PostHog (an analytics tool) so we can see what's working. We don't attach your name or email to it.

Services we use: Clerk (sign-in), Supabase (database and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic (AI), PostHog (analytics), Resend (reminder and alert emails), MailerLite (email list). If you search by ZIP code, it's used only to sort internships by distance: it sits in the page address and your browser, never in your account. Company logos load straight from each company's own website or Google's public icon service, so those see a normal image request (no account info). We don't sell your data.

Your resume link

  • A new upload starts private. Turn the QR link on in Career Fair Mode when you want recruiters to scan it.
  • Each scan opens the file through a link that expires after 2 minutes, so turning sharing off takes effect within about 2 minutes.
  • If your link got passed around, tap Make a new link: the old QR stops working.
  • Delete my resume removes the file and its link for good.

What goes to AI

AI features are optional and only run when you tap them. The text needed for that feature is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer:

  • Company brief: the company's name, website and job titles (nothing about you).
  • Follow-up email: the company, recruiter's name, roles and your notes (not their email address).
  • Homepage tools (cover letter, resume score, interview prep, starter resume, “why it fits”): the answers and resume text you entered for that tool.
  • Tailor resume (on Jobs): the resume text and job description you paste, plus the job's title and company. We don't store it; your pasted resume stays in your browser for next time.
  • Community posts: each post is checked by AI before it's shown.

Resume scores and match percentages are our estimates to help you improve — not a prediction from any employer. AI can be wrong, so read everything before you send it.

Shared or school computers

Sign out when you're done: signing out on the homepage clears this browser's saved quiz, tracker and profile. If you used the homepage without signing in, that data stays in this browser until you clear it.

Delete everything

Signed in? Go to My Season → Delete all my data. That removes your resume file, saved jobs, notes, applications, Orbit connections and messages (for both people in the conversation) and synced homepage data right away.

To also close the sign-in account itself, or for any question, use the Report a problem button on any page and choose “Other”. We'll do it and confirm.
