Privacy Policy, in plain words

What we keep, where it lives, what goes to AI, who can see your resume, and how to delete all of it. Last updated November 12, 2026.

Browsing needs nothing

You can browse every internship without an account or an email. We count page visits and button taps with PostHog (an analytics tool) so we can see what's working. We don't attach your name or email to it. In Europe it stays off until you say yes, and anywhere you can turn it off with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Details: Cookie Policy.

Your resume link

  • A new upload starts private. Turn the QR link on in Career Fair Mode when you want recruiters to scan it.
  • Each scan opens the file through a link that expires after 2 minutes, so turning sharing off takes effect within about 2 minutes.
  • If your link got passed around, tap Make a new link: the old QR stops working.
  • Delete my resume removes the file and its link for good.

What goes to AI

AI features are optional and only run when you tap them. The text needed for that feature is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer:

  • Company brief: the company's name, website and job titles (nothing about you).
  • Follow-up email: the company, recruiter's name, roles and your notes (not their email address).
  • Homepage tools (cover letter, resume score, interview prep, starter resume, “why it fits”): the answers and resume text you entered for that tool.
  • Tailor resume (on Jobs): the resume text and job description you paste, plus the job's title and company. We don't store it; your pasted resume stays in your browser for next time.
  • Community posts: each post is checked by AI before it's shown.

Resume scores and match percentages are our estimates to help you improve, not a prediction from any employer. AI can be wrong, so read everything before you send it.

Shared or school computers

Sign out when you're done: signing out on the homepage clears this browser's saved quiz, tracker and profile. If you used the homepage without signing in, that data stays in this browser until you clear it.

Delete everything

Signed in? Go to My Season → Delete all my data. That removes your resume file, saved jobs, notes, applications, Orbit connections and messages (for both people in the conversation) and synced homepage data right away.

To also close the sign-in account itself, or for any question, use the Feedback button on any page and choose “Other”. We'll do it and confirm.

Who we are

Beat the Pile (www.beatthepile.com) is a service of CuzzoCore LLC, a US limited liability company owned by the founders of Beat the Pile. We decide how the information described here is used. Contact us with the Feedback button on any page (choose “Other”).

Why we use your information

  • To run the features you ask for: saving jobs, your profile, Orbit, reminders, AI tools.
  • To keep the site safe: verifying employers, stopping spam and abuse, limiting how fast tools can be used.
  • To improve it: analytics counts, only as described in the Cookie Policy.
  • To email you: only things you turned on (reminders, job alerts, the quiz email list), each with an unsubscribe link.

We don't sell or rent your information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use it to make automated decisions about you that have legal effects. Scores and match percentages are suggestions, never decisions.

For visitors in Europe, the legal bases are: carrying out our agreement with you (running your account and the features you use), your consent (analytics, the email list, a public profile), and our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure.

Who can use Beat the Pile

You need to be 16 or older to create an account. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you think someone under 16 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data stays until you delete it (My Season → Delete all my data) or ask us to close your account.
  • Data kept only in your browser stays until you sign out, clear it, or clear your browser.
  • Our service providers keep short-term technical logs (for example, server and security logs) under their own retention rules.
  • Problem reports you send us are kept until they're resolved, and deleted on request.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See the information we hold about you, or get a copy of it.
  • Correct it. Most of it you can edit yourself on your profile and in My Season.
  • Delete it. Delete all my data does this right away; we can also close the sign-in account.
  • Stop analytics (Cookie settings), emails (unsubscribe link), or being found (turn off Public profile or “Let employers find me”).

Ask with the Feedback button. We'll confirm the request comes from your account, then answer within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows), free of charge. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, other US states, the EU or the UK), the law may give you more rights, such as appealing our answer or complaining to your data protection authority. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Resume files sit in a private file store and are only reachable through links that expire after 2 minutes. Only the founders can reach the admin tools, and they sign in to do so. No system is perfectly secure. If we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Where it's stored

Beat the Pile is run from the United States, and our providers process data in the US (and may use other countries for backups or support). If you use Beat the Pile from outside the US, your information is transferred there, under the safeguards our providers offer for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll show a notice on the site before the change takes effect and update the date at the top.
