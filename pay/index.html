    Where these numbers come from

    Our Pay Finder bot reads the pay employers put in their own job ads (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards, about 2,300 companies) once a day, and keeps it only for jobs that are open on Beat the Pile. States like California, Colorado, New York and Washington require pay in job ads, so those show up more. A range counts at its midpoint; yearly pay is turned into hourly at 2,080 hours a year. US dollars only. Bonuses, housing and relocation money are not counted. A job with no posted pay is simply left out, so this is a sample, not every job. Those three systems are popular with tech and finance companies, so the sample leans toward higher-paying fields; hospitals, retailers and government jobs mostly use other systems and aren't counted here yet.
