In one paragraph

Beat the Pile (beatthepile.com) helps college students in every major find internships and first jobs straight from employers' own job boards. Its robots read thousands of company career sites every few hours and only show a board after proving it belongs to that company, so every listing links to the employer's real application. Students also get Career Fair Mode, an application tracker, interview practice and Orbit, a network of classmates and students who interned where they want to go. It was started in fall 2026 by Virginia Tech students Adam Shiloni and Maddox Potter and is free for students.
