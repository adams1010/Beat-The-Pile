Finding jobs

Where do the jobs come from?

Straight from each company's own job board (Workday, Greenhouse, iCIMS, Lever and more). Our robots read those boards every few hours, so you see the posting the company itself put up, not a repost on another job site.

We only show a company's board once our robot has proven it belongs to that company, for example because the company's own website links to it.

Is Beat the Pile only for engineers?

No. Every major is covered: business, nursing, psychology, education, communications, design and the rest. Pick your major on the Jobs page, or browse every major.

What's the difference between Internships, Entry-level, Part-time and Shadowing?

Internships are summer, semester and co-op roles. Entry-level are first full-time jobs that don't ask for experience (new grad programs, "Analyst I", nurse residencies). Part-time & hourly are flexible jobs you can work around class. Shadowing & volunteer lets you see a job up close first. Switch between them with the Job type button on Jobs.

How do I search near me?

Type a city, state or ZIP in the Near box on Jobs. You can add up to 5 places and pick a radius from 10 to 200 miles. Remote jobs that can be done from anywhere in the US are included when you filter by state.

Why do some jobs show pay and others don't?

A pay tag means the employer wrote the pay in the job ad. Our Pay Finder bot reads it every day. If the employer didn't post pay, we don't guess. Turn on "$ Shows pay" on Jobs to see only those, and see what internships pay on Internship pay.

A link is broken or a listing looks wrong. What do I do?

Tap "Report" on the job card, or the "?" button at the bottom of any page. Our Link Checker also opens every listing twice a day and hides dead ones after two failed checks in a row.

A company I want isn't on Beat the Pile.

Use the "?" button and pick "Missing a company". Our Company Finder bot looks for their job board and adds it once it can prove the board is theirs. Some systems (chat-bot hiring like Paradox) can't be read yet.

How do I track my applications?

Tap "Mark applied" on any job, or add rows yourself in the Tracker, a spreadsheet with follow-up, interview and offer boxes. My Season shows the same records as a pipeline with a weekly goal.

When do companies open their internships?

The Opening Radar shows when each company usually opened internships in past seasons, so you can be ready on day one. Those dates are estimates from past years and are labeled that way.

Can I get new jobs by email?

Yes. Weekly job alerts for your major are on when you leave the box ticked at sign-up, and you can change them any time in Profile, Settings, Emails. We send at most one a week and none when nothing is new. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

Account & profile

Does it cost anything?

No. Students are never charged. Pro features are part of the beta, and the first 150 student accounts are founding members who keep every Pro feature for life.

How do I sign up?

Go to Sign in, enter your email, and pick Personal account. Step 3 asks for your school and major so classmates can find you. You need to be at least 16.

How do I fill my profile from my resume?

On your profile, choose "Fill your profile from your resume" and upload a PDF or Word file. It fills only empty fields and adds new experience, projects and skills. You can edit everything after.

Who can see my profile?

New profiles are public so classmates can find you in Orbit. Turn Public off on your profile to hide it. Employers only see you if you also turn on "Let employers find me", and only verified employers can message you. Your email and phone show only to signed-in members, and only if you leave that switch on.

How do I change my language or the look of the site?

Tap your picture in the top right. Language has English, Español, Français, Deutsch and Nederlands. Appearance has Default, Light and Dark.

What are badges like "Recruiter" or "Professor"?

They're earned, never self-declared. A Recruiter badge needs a confirmed work email and an owner check. A faculty or staff badge needs an email on the school's own domain plus a link to the school directory, then an owner check.

Privacy & data

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, Settings, Delete account and type DELETE. That removes your profile, saved jobs, tracker, messages, resume files and your sign-in. It can't be undone.

Is my resume shared?

Not unless you turn it on. Uploaded resumes start private. If you turn on the QR link for career fairs, the QR hands out a link that expires after 2 minutes, and you can make a new QR (killing the old one) or delete the file at any time.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell or share personal data for ads. Analytics only run if you accept them, and they don't include your name or email. Details are in the privacy policy and cookie policy.

Someone sent me a job offer that seems fake.

Read Safe job search. We never text job offers, never ask for your SSN or bank details, and never charge you. In Orbit you can block and report anyone from the chat.

Career fairs

What is Career Fair Mode?

Career Fair opens the next fair at your school with its employer list, lets you star companies, builds a route, gives you a short brief on each company, a QR code for your resume, notes per booth, and follow-up emails afterwards.

My school's fair isn't listed.

Tell us with the "?" button and include the fair's page on your school's website. We add fairs only from the school's own page.

Orbit & messages

What is Orbit?

Orbit is the people side: classmates in your major and school, students who interned at companies you want, and people you've connected with. Join someone's orbit, and once they accept you can message each other.

How do I block or report someone?

Open the chat with them and choose Block or Report. Blocking stops requests, messages and search between you. Reports go to the owners with the recent messages.

For employers

How do I set up a company page?

Go to Sign in and choose "Set up my company page". You'll confirm a work email on your company's domain, then an owner reviews it. Only verified companies can search talent, message students or post jobs.

I'm a recruiter. Do I need a business account?

No. Use a personal account and earn a Recruiter badge on your profile with your work email. It links you to your company page and unlocks Find talent.

What do career fair packages include?

See Packages. Featured and Spotlight put your company at the top of a fair's page on Beat the Pile with your open roles. We don't sell fair registration and nothing implies a school's endorsement. Packages are refundable before they go live.

Our jobs are on Beat the Pile but something is wrong.

Email support@beatthepile.com from your company email. We read your public job board, so changes there show up within a few hours.

How our data works

How fresh are the listings?

Every verified board is read every few hours. A job that hasn't been seen on its board for 7 days is hidden until a read finds it again, and dead links are hidden after two failed checks. See the status page for the latest read.

Are the match scores and dates exact?

No, and they say so. Match %, interview chance, resume scores, Radar opening dates and "Posted ~" dates are estimates and are labeled that way. Pay tags and job counts are real data.

Can I use your numbers in an article or class project?

Yes, please cite Beat the Pile and link the page. Hiring pulse and Internship pay update daily, and the press page has more.
