Community guidelines

Beat the Pile works because the people and jobs on it are real. These rules cover your profile, Orbit, messages, the community wall and company pages. They're part of our Terms of use. Last updated November 21, 2026.

Be real

  • Use your real name and your own photo. One personal account per person.
  • Only list schools, majors, jobs and skills that are true. Don't claim a badge, title or company you don't have.
  • Company pages and Recruiter badges are for people who actually work there, confirmed with a work email at that company's domain.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, threats, hate or bullying, in messages, posts or anywhere else.
  • No sexual content, and no unwanted romantic messages. Orbit is for careers.
  • If someone doesn't reply or ignores your Join orbit request, leave it there. Don't keep messaging them.

Keep it safe

  • No scams: no fake job offers, no asking anyone for money, gift cards, bank details or their Social Security number.
  • Don't share someone else's private information (their phone, email, address or resume) without their permission.
  • No malware, phishing links, or attempts to get into someone else's account.

Keep it useful

  • No spam: no mass messages, chain posts, or promoting things that have nothing to do with internships and jobs.
  • Employers: message students about real roles at your company, only students who chose "Let employers find me", and stop if they ask you to.
  • Don't copy other people's posts or profiles, or use the site to collect people's details in bulk.

What happens when someone breaks these

  • Report it. Use Report on a profile or in a chat, or the Report a problem button on any page. Reporting someone from a chat also blocks them for you.
  • We review every report. Depending on what happened, we may remove the content, hide a profile, remove a company page or badge, or suspend the account.
  • Posts on the community wall are checked before they show up, and go back for review if several people report them.
  • Think we got it wrong? Email support@beatthepile.com.

Something off with a job or a recruiter? See Safe job search.
